How to play

  • WASDRun. You control the player with the ring round their feet.
  • ShiftSprint. It uses stamina, which comes back while you jog.
  • EPass along the ground to the teammate you're facing, or the way you're pressing.
  • QLofted pass, over the defenders' heads.
  • SpaceShoot. Hold it and the power bar fills (past 100% it flies over the bar). Let go and the strike needle runs. Press it again while the needle's in the green. The gold middle is a perfect strike. Too early and you sky it, too late and you scuff it. Hold W or S as you strike to pick a post.
  • TSlide tackle. Get the ball and it's yours to chase. Get the player and not the ball and it's a free kick.
  • CSwitch player. Press it again to go to the next nearest.
  • PPause. M mutes.

On a phone or tablet

Put your left thumb anywhere on the left side and move it to run. The buttons on the right do the rest: Shoot works like Space (hold, let go, tap). Buttons that don't do anything right now are dimmed.

Two on one keyboard

Blues: arrow keys run, Right Shift sprint, K pass, J lob, L shoot, U slide, I switch. Reds use the keys above, with Left Shift to sprint.

Kick-offs and restarts

At a kick-off the other team stays in its own half, out of the centre circle, until you play the ball. On a kick-off, throw-in or corner you have to pass it. On a free kick you can shoot.
